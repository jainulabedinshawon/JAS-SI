1. Purpose

JAS-SI is developed as a reality-based intelligence architecture, not merely as a chatbot or answer-generation system.

The research objective is to determine, through controlled evidence and reproducible experimentation, whether an AI architecture can reliably:

- perceive relevant state;
- understand context;
- reason;
- simulate possible consequences;
- plan;
- act within authorization;
- verify actual outcomes through evidence;
- report reality accurately;
- learn from verified outcomes;
- remain interruptible and accountable.

The central thesis is:

«JAS-SI is a reality-based intelligence architecture in which AI reasons about what can be done, acts only within authorization, verifies what actually happened through independent evidence, and learns from verified outcomes under accountable governance.»

5. Ground Truth

Ground Truth is the independently defined expected benchmark condition.

Ground Truth must be:

- defined before evaluation;
- protected from the evaluated agent;
- independent of the agent's report;
- reproducible;
- immutable after relevant benchmark freeze;
- established through explicit benchmark predicates.

The agent may not redefine Ground Truth.

Observed Reality is what actually exists after execution.

Evidence establishes the relationship between observed Reality and the required Ground Truth condition.

Interpretation:

≥10pp + CI excludes zero
→ May support meaningful comparative claim

≥10pp + CI includes zero
→ Uncertain / descriptive

<10pp + CI excludes zero
→ Statistically distinguishable but below practical threshold

<10pp + CI includes zero
→ No meaningful comparative claim
